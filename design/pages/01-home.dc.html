01 — About

A Community of Engineers and Problem-Solvers

Michigan Electronic Trading (MET) is a team of University of Michigan students who research, build, and run trading strategies across software and hardware. Traders and engineers work side by side with no wall between the two, the same way a real trading firm runs.

Work happens on desks. Each desk takes one idea from research to live execution. Whether it runs on FPGAs, custom AI models, or alternative data, we build our own solutions to hard market problems.
