01 — Tracks
Track A

Trading Track

Research and run the strategies our desks trade.

What we look for
  • Comfort with probability and quick mental math
  • Curiosity about how markets work
  • Coding experience in Python or similar
Track B

Engineering Track

Build the software and hardware our desks run on.

What we look for
  • Strong programming, especially C++ or Python
  • Interest in low-latency systems, FPGAs, or networking
  • Projects you've built on your own
02 — Process
  1. 01 → Winterfest [DATE] Find us at our table to meet members and ask questions.
  2. 02 → Mass Meeting [DATE] Hear how MET works, what desks are building, and how to apply.
  3. 03 → Application [DATE] A short written application. Pick a track and tell us what you've built.
  4. 04 → Interviews [DATE] Behavioral and technical rounds. Final rounds include a live trading game.
03 — FAQ
Do I need trading experience?+

No. New members go through a weekly education program that covers the basics.

Can freshmen apply?+

Yes. All years and majors can apply.

Can I apply to both tracks?+

Pick one when you apply. Desks mix both tracks, so you'll work with the other side either way.
